## 🎬 Writer's Final Scene:  The Mystery of the Time Traveler (10대 시네마틱 헌법)

**[Fade In]**:

**(Scene fades in, with a close-up on the writer, who looks directly into the camera)**

**Writer:** "오늘은 그날이었다. 당신의 눈물에 달려 있던 시간.  ...정말 어떤 일을 떠올리며 기다렸는지."

**(The scene zooms out to show a vintage video of a bustling 1997 Seoul)**

**Writer:** "24시간 전, 대한민국은 국가 부도를 선언했다. 환전소 장부에 적힌 그녀의 서명은 미스터리. 그때, 누구였다?  (pause for effect) 지갑 속 달러를 확인해보세요." 


**(Scene cuts to a quick sequence of images: an old-fashioned cell phone falling in slow motion; the sound of coins clinking together; a young woman's face appearing with hope and doubt)**

**Writer:** "그녀가 환전을 마치고 골목 너머로 사라진 직후, 정확히 아침 9시.  (dramatic pause) 대한민국 경제는 무너지기."

**(Scene cuts to a black screen for a second; then transitions back to the writer)** 


**Writer:** (Voice starts rising in intensity and urgency) "정작에 그녀의 이미지, 시간 여행을 시작하고... 그 시대가 다시 어디로 가든, 왜?"  (pause for effect, look at camera)

**(Scene cuts to a series of fast-paced images: an exploding stock market chart; the sound of a gunshot; news footage showing riots and protests)**


**Writer:** "시네마틱은 당신의 시선만큼으로! 댓글을 남겨 주세요!" (pause for effect, look at camera)

**(Scene cuts to a close-up on the writer's eyes with an expression of suspense. Fade out.)**



##  Notes:


* **Time & Space:** Utilize time manipulation techniques in a way that feels surreal and impactful; e.g., quick flashes and zooms.
* **Sound Design:** Sound design is crucial! Use sound effects, music cues, and Foley to create an immersive experience. 
* **Visuals:**  Use dynamic camera movement and visual transitions (zoom, pan) for a captivating effect. 


**Remember**: The goal is to leave the audience with questions that will make them engage in comments. Create tension and keep the viewers on the edge of their seats!
